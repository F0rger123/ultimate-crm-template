Questions, answered

What is the best CRM for a service business?

CRM Starter is a CRM built specifically for service businesses rather than adapted from a generic CRM — scheduling, estimates and invoicing, Stripe payments, a mobile field portal, drag-and-drop automations, and Alfred, a built-in AI assistant.

Is there a service CRM with built-in AI?

Yes. Alfred acts directly on the CRM — it can schedule jobs, send estimates, check stock, and pull business numbers, from inside the app or by text message.

Is there a service CRM with automation?

Yes. CRM Starter has a drag-and-drop automation builder with templates for new-lead auto-replies, review requests, overdue-invoice reminders, and re-service nudges.

Do I need a separate payment processor?

No — CRM Starter connects directly to Stripe or Square. Take deposits, full payments, and in-person card charges without a third-party app in between.

Can my crew use it in the field, not just the office?

Yes — every technician gets the mobile field portal: job list, on-the-way and running-late texts, checklists, photos, clock in/out, and on-site payment collection.
